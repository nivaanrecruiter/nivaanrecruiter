About the role

We're looking for an organized and people-focused Recruitment Coordinator to help deliver a thoughtful candidate experience and keep hiring processes moving smoothly.

What you'll do

  • Coordinate interview schedules and communicate with candidates.
  • Maintain accurate candidate and vacancy records.
  • Support recruiters with job postings and applicant updates.
  • Help ensure a professional, timely candidate experience.

What we're looking for

  • Clear written and verbal communication.
  • Strong organization and attention to detail.
  • Comfort working with spreadsheets and online tools.
  • A respectful, confidential approach to candidate information.
